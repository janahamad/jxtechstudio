Picking an Agency

3 min read

Questions to Ask a Web Development Agency Before You Sign Anything

Choosing a web development agency isn't just about comparing prices. You're choosing a long-term technical partner — one that could shape the foundation of your business for years. The wrong choice can mean missed deadlines, hidden costs, poor-quality code, or a project you can't even maintain after it's delivered. Before signing anything, make sure you ask the right questions.

1. Who Owns the Code?

This should be one of the first questions you ask. When the project is complete, clarify whether you'll receive full source code, database files, design assets, documentation, server access, and domain ownership. You should never discover after launch that you're locked out of your own project.

2. What Technology Stack Will You Use?

You don't need every technical detail, but you should understand the basics: which frontend framework, which backend, which database, and why this stack is the right fit for your project. A good agency can explain their choices in plain language, not just jargon.

3. How Will the Project Be Managed?

Successful projects depend on communication. Ask how often you'll get updates, who your main point of contact is, how changes are handled, and which project management tools they use. Clear communication reduces misunderstandings and keeps things on schedule.

4. What Happens If Requirements Change?

Almost every project evolves during development. Ask how the agency handles additional features, scope changes, timeline adjustments, and budget updates. A transparent change process helps avoid unpleasant surprises later.

5. Is the Website Optimized for Performance?

A beautiful website isn't enough if it's slow. Ask whether the agency considers performance, mobile responsiveness, image optimization, caching, and Core Web Vitals. Fast websites improve both user experience and business results.

6. What About Security?

Security shouldn't be an afterthought. Ask whether the project includes HTTPS configuration, secure authentication, input validation, regular security updates, and protection against common web vulnerabilities. Even small business websites become targets for automated attacks.

7. How Will the Website Be Deployed?

Deployment is often overlooked until launch day. Ask where the application will be hosted, how updates are deployed, whether there's a staging environment, and whether deployments can be rolled back if something goes wrong.

8. Will the Website Be Easy to Maintain?

Your business will grow, and your website should grow with it. Ask about clean, maintainable code, documentation, an admin panel if needed, and future scalability. Building for the future saves significant time and money down the road.

9. What Happens After Launch?

Launch day isn't the end of the project. Ask whether the agency provides bug fixes, maintenance plans, monitoring, backups, and technical support. A good agency keeps supporting your business after delivery — not just until the invoice is paid.

Happy to answer all of these for your project — no pressure.
