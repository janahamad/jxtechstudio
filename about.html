About

Who We Are

JXTech Studio is a small, independent web development studio. We design and build custom web applications and digital products for businesses that need software built right — and we build our own products alongside client work, so we stay close to the tools and practices we recommend to others.

What We Do

We work across the full lifecycle of a digital product: turning an idea into a scoped plan, building the application with a modern, maintainable stack, and staying involved after launch to fix issues and ship improvements. Most of our work falls into three areas — web development, SaaS products, and brand & UI design.

Why We Build Our Own Products

Alongside client projects, we build and operate our own software — starting with BoostSEO, a free technical SEO audit tool we designed, built, and maintain ourselves. Running a live product means we deal firsthand with the same problems our clients face: uptime, performance, real users, and the slow accumulation of edge cases that only show up in production. That experience directly shapes how we scope and build client work.

How We Work

We keep engagements small and direct — no account managers, no hand-offs between sales and delivery. You talk to the person who is actually writing the code, from the first scoping call through to launch and support. We favor shipping in small, visible increments over long stretches of silence followed by a single big reveal.

Get in Touch

If you have a project in mind, the fastest way to reach us is through the contact page or directly at info@jxtechstudio.com.
